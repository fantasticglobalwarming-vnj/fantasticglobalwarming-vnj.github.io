Module 1 – What Is Deforestation?

Deforestation is the large-scale removal of forests, usually to make land available for agriculture, settlements, or industrial use. It involves cutting down trees faster than forests can naturally regrow. Forests play a crucial role in absorbing carbon dioxide, regulating climate, and supporting ecosystems. When forests are removed, these functions are lost. Deforestation is a major environmental issue with global consequences.
